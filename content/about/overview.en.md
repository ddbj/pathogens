{{< paragraph title="About the portal">}}
This portal is a part of [Pathogen Data Network](https://pathogendatanetwork.org), a global consortium coordinated by the [SIB (Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics](https://www.sib.swiss/), and is operated by [DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ)](https://www.ddbj.nig.ac.jp).
The website is built using Hugo on GitHub based on the theme of [SciLifeLab Data Centre](https://scilifelab.se/data/).
We make all our codes open source under the MIT license. 
{{< /paragraph >}}

{{< paragraph title="History">}}
An original portal was launched by [RCOS](https://rcos.nii.ac.jp/)
(Research Center for Open Science and Data Platform, National Institute of Informatics) as "COVID-19 Data Portal Japan" in 2020 in collaboration with other COVID-19 data portals.
The current version started as a DDBJ service in 2025.
{{< /paragraph >}}
